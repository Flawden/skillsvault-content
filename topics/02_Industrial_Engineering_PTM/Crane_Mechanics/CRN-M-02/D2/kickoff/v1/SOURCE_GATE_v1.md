# CRN-M-02 / D2 — Source Gate v1

## Decision

**PASS WITH CLAIM LIMITS**

D2 lesson authoring is unlocked for applied qualitative mechanics, model-selection reasoning, system-geometry interpretation and escalation boundaries.

Numeric design/acceptance criteria, standard-derived selection factors and equipment-specific corrective actions remain locked.

## 1. ISO 4306-1:2026

`Cranes — Vocabulary — Part 1: General`

Verified 2026-09-02 from ISO public metadata:

- Edition 5;
- published 2026-07;
- status: Published;
- covers common crane terminology, parameters, concepts and component parts.

Role in D2: terminology/component anchor only.

Public metadata: https://www.iso.org/standard/78820.html

## 2. ISO 16625:2025

`Cranes and hoists — Selection of wire ropes, drums and sheaves`

Verified 2026-09-02:

- Edition 2;
- published 2025-02;
- status: Published;
- supersedes withdrawn ISO 16625:2013;
- public abstract explicitly states that rope-drive geometry and drum/sheave geometry are incorporated in proof of competence / selection.

Role in D2: primary engineering boundary proving that geometry and component compatibility belong to the engineering selection problem.

Restriction: public metadata/abstract is insufficient to reproduce paid tables, exact factors, diameters, groove geometry or other numerical criteria.

Public metadata: https://www.iso.org/standard/77889.html

## 3. ISO 4301-1:2016

`Cranes — Classification — Part 1: General`

Verified 2026-09-02:

- Edition 3;
- status: Published / Confirmed;
- ISO reports the 2021 systematic review as confirmed and the version remains current;
- public abstract ties classification to working cycles, load spectrum and average displacements.

Role in D2: duty/classification context. D2 may explain that service classification matters to engineering selection, but MUST NOT infer or reproduce mechanism-specific selection factors not available in the public source.

Public metadata: https://www.iso.org/standard/63070.html

## 4. ISO 4309:2017

`Cranes — Wire ropes — Care and maintenance, inspection and discard`

Verified 2026-09-02:

- current published Edition 5;
- public abstract explicitly identifies multilayer-spooling crossover zones as locations where deterioration can be significantly greater;
- applies to rope on cranes/hoists and hoist blocks.

Role in D2: applied rope/drum/sheave condition interface and qualitative multilayer/crossover risk anchor.

Restriction: no discard numbers/criteria are reproduced from paid text.

Public metadata: https://www.iso.org/standard/66759.html

## 5. ISO/CD 4309 — Edition 6

Verified 2026-09-02:

- status: Under development;
- Committee Draft consultation stage 30.20.

Role: **watchlist only**. It is not current published normative authority and must not replace ISO 4309:2017 in D2 claims.

Public metadata: https://www.iso.org/standard/90256.html

## 6. ФНП ПС №461

`Приказ Ростехнадзора от 26.11.2020 №461`.

Verified 2026-09-02:

- current text: редакция 16.04.2026;
- validity limited through 01.09.2032;
- contains operational, inspection/replacement and equipment-element safety boundaries for lifting structures.

Role in D2: Russian regulatory/operational boundary. It does not convert D2 into a field-repair or redesign manual.

Reference: https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_373321/

## 7. Passport / OEM documentation

Required for actual equipment:

- reeving and dead-end arrangement;
- drum/sheave dimensions and groove data;
- permissible rope type/size and compatibility;
- fleet/lead geometry limits;
- spooling/pretension/installation procedure;
- component acceptance, repair, replacement or modification decisions;
- any equipment-specific efficiency, capacity or service limit.

## D2 authoring guards

D2 MUST NOT invent or generalize:

- D/d minimums or selection factors;
- numerical groove geometry;
- numerical fleet/deflection-angle limits;
- numerical efficiency/loss coefficients;
- allowable drum/sheave stresses or contact pressures;
- discard/acceptance limits;
- equipment-specific rope or reeving instructions.

If a concept requires one of these values, teach the dependency and model-status boundary while leaving the exact criterion source-dependent.

## Result

The source set is sufficient for a safe D2 applied-mechanics lesson under explicit numeric/OEM claim locks.

**D2 SOURCE GATE = COMPLETE / PASS WITH CLAIM LIMITS.**
