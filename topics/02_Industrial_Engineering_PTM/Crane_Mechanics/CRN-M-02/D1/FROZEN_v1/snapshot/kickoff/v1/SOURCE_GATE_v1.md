## 1. ISO 4306-1:2026

`Cranes — Vocabulary — Part 1: General`

Public ISO metadata verified 2026-09-01:

- Edition 5;
- published 2026-07;
- current status: published;
- establishes commonly used crane vocabulary and component-part terminology.

Role in D1: terminology and component-role anchor.

Public metadata URL: https://www.iso.org/standard/78820.html

## 2. ISO 16625:2025

`Cranes and hoists — Selection of wire ropes, drums and sheaves`

Public ISO metadata verified 2026-09-01:

- Edition 2;
- published 2025-02;
- current status: published;
- ISO 16625:2013 is withdrawn;
- public abstract explicitly places rope-drive geometry and drum/sheave geometry inside the selection problem.

Role in D1: primary engineering boundary showing that drum/sheave geometry is part of rope-system compatibility.

Claim restriction: public metadata is not enough to reproduce paid tables, selection factors or exact numeric limits.

Public metadata URL: https://www.iso.org/standard/77889.html

## 3. ФНП ПС №461

`Приказ Ростехнадзора от 26.11.2020 №461`.

Verified status 2026-09-01:

- current text includes amendments of 22.01.2024 №16 and 16.04.2026 №129;
- effective document remains the Russian industrial-safety regulatory anchor for OPO using lifting structures;
- validity is limited through 01.09.2032.

Role in D1: regulatory/documentation hierarchy and operational safety boundary. It does not replace the design standard or the specific equipment manual for technical dimensions.

Reference: https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_373321/

## 4. ISO 4309:2017

`Cranes — Wire ropes — Care and maintenance, inspection and discard`.

Verified 2026-09-01:

- published edition remains available;
- a new edition is under development in ISO/TC 96/SC 3;
- scope includes rope on cranes/hoists and hoist blocks.

Role in D1: supporting interface source for understanding that drum/sheave/rope interaction matters to rope condition and inspection context.

Watchlist: recheck before any freshness/freeze step because revision activity is active.

Public metadata URL: https://www.iso.org/standard/66759.html

## 5. Passport / OEM documentation

Required for:

- actual reeving scheme;
- rope anchoring and dead-end arrangement;
- actual drum/sheave dimensions and groove data;
- permitted rope types/sizes;
- equipment-specific service, inspection and replacement limits;
- any modification or field acceptance decision.

This source is equipment-specific and is not assumed to be publicly distributable.

## Authoring guards

D1 authoring MUST NOT invent:

- D/d minimums;
- groove radii/pitch/depth;
- fleet-angle numerical limits;
- drum capacity formulas presented as equipment limits;
- design factors;
- acceptance/discard limits;
- OEM-specific reeving or service limits.

If a useful D1 explanation would normally use one of these values, teach the concept without the number and explicitly mark the exact criterion as source-dependent.

The source set is sufficient to author a safe beginner D1 mental model.
