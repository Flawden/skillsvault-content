# CRN-M-02 / D1 Practice v1 — Alignment Map

## Purpose

Practice A–H verifies transfer from the reviewed D1 lesson into diagram reading and bounded idealized reasoning.

It does not introduce a new technical layer. Every exercise remains inside the locked D1 scope/source/field-authority boundaries.

## Case alignment

| Case | Lesson anchors | Main competence |
|---|---|---|
| A | §§1–4 | distinguish component count from rope-path/force analysis |
| B | §3, §9 | fixed-sheave trap / identify actual support |
| C | §§4–5 | full rope-path → force-map → simple `n` workflow |
| D | §§5–7 | ideal force, travel and speed reciprocity |
| E | §4, §9 | moving dead-end attachment / force-transfer map |
| F | §4 stop signals, §9 | refuse unjustified simple model for compound/multi-body scheme |
| G | §§6–7 | ideal kinematics and ideal-vs-real boundary |
| H | §§10–11 | diagram comprehension vs field authority/conformance |

## Required D1 capabilities

Across A–H the learner must demonstrate:

1. moving-assembly identification;
2. rope-endpoint identification;
3. continuous rope-path tracing;
4. force-transfer mapping;
5. correct handling of a moving dead end;
6. fixed-vs-moving block reasoning in the chosen frame;
7. explicit simple-model applicability check;
8. symbolic/elementary ideal mechanics only after applicability is established;
9. travel/speed reciprocity for a confirmed simple scheme;
10. stop/escalation behavior for compound or ambiguous schemes;
11. separation of diagram comprehension from real-equipment authority;
12. refusal to invent numeric design/acceptance limits.
