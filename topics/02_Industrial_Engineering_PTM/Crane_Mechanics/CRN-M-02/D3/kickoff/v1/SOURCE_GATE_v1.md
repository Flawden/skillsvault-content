## 11. Passport / OEM / actual equipment records

Required for equipment-specific claims and for discriminating many real causal hypotheses:

- approved reeving and geometry;
- drum/sheave dimensions and material/configuration;
- rope type and compatibility;
- drive/brake/control characteristics;
- service/duty records and event logs where available;
- installation/maintenance history;
- permitted limits, inspection/repair procedures and modifications.

## D3 authoring guards

D3 MUST NOT invent or generalize:

- ISO 8686 dynamic factors or load combinations;
- ISO 16625 proof/selection factors, D/d values or geometry limits;
- numerical efficiency/loss coefficients as equipment values;
- numerical contact/stress/bearing limits;
- fatigue-life or remaining-life numbers;
- actual design-working-period conclusion;
- discard/acceptance limits from paid text;
- NDT/MRT interpretation beyond qualified method boundaries;
- equipment-specific repair, rerouting, redesign or life-extension instructions.

D3 may teach the dependency, causal model, sensitivity direction, evidence requirement and stop/escalation rule while leaving exact criteria source-dependent.

## Result

The source set is sufficient for a safe D3 causal-analysis lesson under explicit proof/design/remaining-life/OEM claim locks.

**D3 SOURCE GATE = COMPLETE / PASS WITH CLAIM LIMITS.**
