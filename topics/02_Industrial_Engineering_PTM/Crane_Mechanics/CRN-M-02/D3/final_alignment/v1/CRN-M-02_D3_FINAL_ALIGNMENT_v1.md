
# 7. U1 + C1 closure audit

The Verification architecture correctly contains three different evidence layers:

1. **Known Case Evidence A–L** — demonstrates working technique for ER-D3-01…12.
2. **Unseen U1** — requires causal transfer in a new mixed-mechanism case and closes the transfer half of ER-D3-13.
3. **Mandatory C1** — changes mapping/event applicability after the learner has committed to an initial status and requires an explicit update of evidence matrix, unknown register, relative support, model status and bounded conclusion.

This is intentional and internally consistent:

```text
Known A–L alone → NOT SUFFICIENT
U1 without C1   → NOT SUFFICIENT
U1 + C1 + all ER >=3 + no BF → D3 VERIFIED
```

C1 does not require guessing a hidden preferred root cause. It evaluates evidence-linked updating. A learner may preserve, weaken, re-rank or invalidate a model if the supplied evidence supports that action.

BF-D3-11 therefore has prior instructional origin (§7, §18, §22–24, §31–32), known-case update patterns and explicit ER-D3-06/13 lineage before becoming non-compensable at C1.

# 8. Guard consistency

The following invariants survive Lesson → Practice → Reasoning → ER → Rubric without weakening:

1. observed signature is separated from explanation;
2. at least two physically meaningful hypotheses remain alive until evidence justifies narrowing;
3. `H_data` is a falsifiable competing hypothesis when data quality/mapping can explain the signature, not a trash bin for inconvenient evidence;
4. negative evidence cannot falsify a model until detectability/coverage/applicability has been checked;
5. a mismatch does not automatically invalidate a model when observability, mapping or event applicability is uncertain;
6. current snapshot and accumulated duty/history are different evidence objects;
7. before/after maintenance is a change bundle unless confounders and baseline conditions justify stronger inference;
8. dynamic/transient states require a rebuilt model; unmeasured acceleration is not silently set to zero;
9. qualitative/directional sensitivity does not identify a causal effect by itself;
10. assumptions and unknowns remain explicit and can legitimately force `DISTINGUISHING DATA REQUIRED`, `SOURCE / OEM / SPECIALIST REQUIRED` or `NOT YET DEMONSTRATED`;
11. evidence roles are question-specific; observation, monitoring/history, OEM/passport/design information and specialist evidence are not collapsed into a universal ranking;
12. source existence is distinct from method applicability, sufficient input data, competence and authority;
13. bounded support is not formal root-cause certification;
14. D3 does not introduce paid-standard factors, proof/load combinations, acceptance/discard thresholds, fatigue/remaining-life numbers or equipment-specific intervention permission;
15. D1 and D2 frozen trees remain immutable prerequisites and their numeric authority is not automatically transferred into D3;
16. Rubric score bands `39–45` / `46–52` are pedagogical assessment policy only and are not crane engineering criteria.
