### B. Generic mechanics

Public-domain engineering mechanics concepts used symbolically:

- vector force balance;
- `ΣF_ext = m·a_G` for a named body in an inertial frame;
- `ΣM_axis = I_axis·α` only for an explicitly scoped planar/fixed-axis rigid-body model;
- cause/effect model construction;
- qualitative directional-sensitivity reasoning; derivative notation only when a mathematical model and held variables are defined;
- distinction between state variables, history variables, assumptions and unknowns;
- Red-Team discipline that bounded support is relative to the evaluated hypothesis set;
- data-quality/artifact hypotheses must themselves be falsifiable;
- negative evidence requires observability and applicability before model invalidation;
- directional sensitivity is a model prediction, not causal identification.

These are not crane-specific proof calculations and do not supply ISO/OEM coefficients.

### C. External published/regulatory anchors from D3 Source Gate

#### ISO 4306-1:2026
Role: vocabulary/component terminology.
Public metadata: https://www.iso.org/standard/78820.html

#### ISO 16625:2025
Role: boundary that rope-drive/drum/sheave selection and geometry belong to formal proof/selection engineering. Lesson does not reconstruct proof factors or numeric criteria.
Public metadata: https://www.iso.org/standard/77889.html

#### ISO 4301-1:2016
Role: duty/classification context; service condition is not reducible to one instantaneous load. No actual class/factor is calculated from public metadata.
Public metadata: https://www.iso.org/standard/63070.html

#### ISO 8686-1:2012
Role: load/dynamics/method boundary. Supports the distinction between generic kinetic/elastostatic reasoning and formal crane load/load-combination proof methods. No dynamic factor or load-combination value is reproduced.
Public metadata: https://www.iso.org/standard/59417.html

#### ISO 4309:2017
Role: rope-condition/degradation evidence boundary and multilayer/crossover deterioration context. No discard table or criterion is reconstructed.
Public metadata: https://www.iso.org/standard/66759.html

#### ISO 9927-1:2013
Role: inspection-evidence boundary. The lesson distinguishes inspection evidence from design/OEM/specialist authority.
Public metadata: https://www.iso.org/standard/51102.html

#### ISO 12482:2014
Role: accumulated duty/monitoring/design-working-period context. The lesson may teach that operating history is evidence distinct from a snapshot; it may not calculate/certify remaining design life. Red Team additionally locks the distinction `source exists ≠ method applicable ≠ data sufficient ≠ authority granted`.
Public metadata: https://www.iso.org/standard/57481.html

#### ФНП ПС №461, current edition 16.04.2026
Role: Russian regulatory/operation/inspection/repair authority boundary. D3 causal analysis does not replace regulatory responsibilities or equipment documentation.
Reference: https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_373321/
