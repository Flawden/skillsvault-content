
# 9. Guard consistency

The following invariants survive Lesson → reviewed Lesson → Practice → Reasoning → ER → Rubric without weakening:

1. decision question and claim class precede method selection;
2. as-found, approved/design and calculation configurations remain separate evidence objects until equivalence is demonstrated;
3. decision-driving unknowns are not silently replaced by nominal/typical values;
4. source existence/value authenticity is separate from current-case branch/method applicability;
5. smallest adequate fidelity is preferred over complexity for its own sake;
6. solver convergence, mesh refinement or higher fidelity do not create validation status;
7. parent→child/local model results require auditable load/boundary/configuration interfaces;
8. verification, cross-check and validation answer distinct questions;
9. agreement of two models does not exclude common-mode configuration/input/source/criterion error;
10. sensitivity/uncertainty is tied to the possibility of changing the decision, not to arbitrary perturbation;
11. robustness is bounded by the provenance and coverage of the scenario/model-form envelope;
12. inspection, monitoring/history, classification, proof, remaining-life and authority evidence retain separate roles;
13. value-of-information prioritizes evidence that can change the decision rather than merely add analysis volume;
14. `STOP / REQUEST EVIDENCE` can be a strong technical outcome when the evidence chain is incomplete;
15. `ANALYSIS STOPPED ≠ OPERATION PERMITTED` remains explicit;
16. technical recommendation does not become equipment-specific approval/permission;
17. paid-source tables/coefficients/branch logic are not reconstructed;
18. D4 does not introduce actual proof/load-combination results, numeric remaining-life, discard/acceptance thresholds or specialist NDT verdicts;
19. D1/D2/D3 frozen trees and `FROZEN_INDEX` remain immutable prerequisites;
