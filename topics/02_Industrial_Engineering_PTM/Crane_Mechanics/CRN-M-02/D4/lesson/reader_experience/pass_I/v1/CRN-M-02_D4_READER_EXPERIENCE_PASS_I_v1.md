# Findings and improvements

## RX-D4-01 — The nine-part architecture was explicit to the author, but not compactly visible to the reader
**Severity:** HIGH

Added a whole-D4 map in Part I and a capstone map in Part IX. The reader now sees one climb from question classification through method/applicability, modelling, V&V, uncertainty and authority handoff.

## RX-D4-02 — Parts lacked a uniform capability target
**Severity:** HIGH

Every Part I–IX now states `К концу части ты должен уметь…`. These are observable capabilities, not chapter summaries.

## RX-D4-03 — Guard-dense sections could make D4 feel like a list of prohibitions
**Severity:** HIGH

Parts II–VIII now start with a positive main causal line showing what the guards protect: boundary formulation, model selection, proof traceability, parent/child interfaces, decision robustness and evidence-channel separation.

## RX-D4-04 — Hybrid source/method terminology created avoidable memorization pressure
**Severity:** MEDIUM

Part I explicitly tells the reader not to memorize standard numbers or every English label. The required skill is to preserve engineering distinctions and restate them accurately. Part IV reinforces the source-role route.

## RX-D4-05 — Operational templates existed, but the reader had no compact entry card for when to use them
**Severity:** HIGH

Reader Navigators now route the reader into the existing configuration, output, source-bound input, parent/child and uncertainty protocols before detailed prose. No technical protocol was removed.

## RX-D4-06 — Verification, cross-check and validation remained cognitively expensive even after technical correction
**Severity:** HIGH

Parts III and VI now expose two independent axes (fidelity vs validation status) and a three-column `verification / cross-check / validation` memory map. The common-mode-error Red-Team guard remains explicit.

## RX-D4-07 — Duty, inspection, monitoring, proof and authority needed one role-first map
**Severity:** HIGH

Part VIII now presents evidence channels by question answered. Channels do not “vote”; conflicts trigger state/scope/observability/method review.

## RX-D4-08 — Retrieval practice was inconsistent across Parts
**Severity:** MEDIUM

Every Part now ends with a Reader Checkpoint that requires reconstruction or explanation without vocabulary-only recall. Existing mini-reviews/checklists remain intact.

## RX-D4-09 — Final synthesis still read partly like another content chapter and contained a stale next-gate sentence
**Severity:** MEDIUM

Part IX now begins as a capstone navigator, adds final retrieval, and correctly routes the post-review pipeline to D4 Practice.

# Guard preservation

Reader Experience Pass I does not weaken these reviewed invariants:

```text
OBSERVED ≠ APPROVED
PLAUSIBLE ≠ APPLICABLE
MODEL FIDELITY ≠ VALIDATION STATUS
SOLVER CONVERGED ≠ MODEL VALIDATED
TWO MODELS AGREE ≠ COMMON-MODE ERROR EXCLUDED
ROBUST IN CHOSEN ENVELOPE ≠ GLOBALLY ROBUST
NO DEFECT OBSERVED ≠ DEFECT ABSENT
ANALYSIS STOPPED ≠ OPERATION PERMITTED
TECHNICAL RECOMMENDATION ≠ AUTHORIZED EQUIPMENT DECISION
```

Still locked:
- closed paid tables, coefficients and proprietary method reconstruction;
- actual source-bound component proof without exact applicable method and inputs;
- numeric remaining-life verdict;
- discard/acceptance criteria imported from secondary sources;
- specialist NDT interpretation;
- equipment-specific repair/redesign/approval or field authority.

## Whole-lesson reader route

```text
CLASSIFY THE QUESTION
→ LOCK CONFIGURATION / STATE / OUTPUT
→ CHOOSE THE SIMPLEST ADEQUATE METHOD
→ PROVE APPLICABILITY + INPUT PROVENANCE
→ CONNECT GLOBAL / LOCAL MODELS
→ VERIFY IMPLEMENTATION
→ VALIDATE / CROSS-CHECK IN DOMAIN
→ CHALLENGE SENSITIVITY + UNCERTAINTY
→ INTEGRATE DUTY / CONDITION EVIDENCE BY ROLE
→ WRITE A BOUNDED REVIEW
→ STOP OR HAND OFF TO THE RIGHT AUTHORITY
```
