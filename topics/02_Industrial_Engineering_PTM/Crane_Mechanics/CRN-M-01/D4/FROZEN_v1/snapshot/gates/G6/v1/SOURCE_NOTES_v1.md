## ISO 7592:1983
Статус:
- Published;
- current after 2021 confirmation;
- currently in review cycle.

Публичная область:
- calibrated round steel link lifting chains;
- chains operating over chain wheels;

- chain ↔ wheel system framing.

## pewag — Don’t Replace the Hoist. Replace the Chain, 01.07.2026
Тип:
- current manufacturer technical/maintenance article.

Использовано:
- fine dimensional tolerances;
- chain and pocket wheel as matched system;
- superficially similar chain may skip/jam/increase wheel wear;
- T / DAT / DT product distinction;
- replacement must be based on hoist manual/certificate.

Guard:
- manufacturer-specific hardness and replacement figures not universalized.

---

## pewag — adjustable individual sprocket teeth
Тип:
- manufacturer product evidence for a different heavy-chain system.

Использовано только как proof-of-mechanism:
- interlink wear changes chain length;
- sprocket geometry may be adjusted in that specific product family.

Guard:
- not applicable as a repair method for chain hoists.

---

## Hiroshima & Sawa, 1995–1996
Серия peer-reviewed работ по link chains in chain hoists.

Подтверждает:
- combined torsion, tension, impact and bending;
- nonuniform local stress fields;
- 3D elastoplastic FE can be compared with experiments;
- fatigue models can be validated for a defined chain/test configuration.

Guard:
- specific crack location, S–N relation and numerical strength effects are not universalized.

---

## Identification and modelling of dynamic parameters for round link chains subject to axial loads, 2022
Тип:
- research article.

Использовано:
- modern validation of dynamic chain modelling;
- dynamic response may require more than static force model.

Guard:
- generic round-link chain model ≠ specific hoist-chain approval.

---

## CM Lodestar manual
Тип:
- OEM manual.

Публично индексируемый текст подтверждает:
- especially thorough inspection/lubrication of the same chain area when travel reversal repeatedly occurs at the same location;
- worn chain can indicate worn guides, hook blocks and liftwheel.

Использовано:
- working-height coordinate;
- coupled component inspection.

Guard:
- numeric limits of a particular CM hoist are not transferred to other hoists.
