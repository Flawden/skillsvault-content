## Evidence outputs

D4 evidence package should include:

1. Decision Question
2. Evidence Ledger
3. Competing Models
4. Source / Method Map
5. Differentiating Evidence Plan
6. Sensitivity / Uncertainty
7. Bounded Conclusion
8. Authority / Safety Boundary

---

## Transfer requirement

Known-case performance alone is insufficient.

Verification must include at least one unseen case where:
- surface story differs from Practice A–H;
- multiple models remain plausible;
- at least one critical datum is missing;
- a source/authority distinction matters;
- the best output may be `NOT DEMONSTRATED` or `INSUFFICIENT DATA`.

---

## Blocking failure candidates for Verification Rubric

These should likely be non-compensable:
- invented critical input;
- explicit safety delay when criterion-based action is already established;
- authority creep;
- false claim of legal/source applicability;
- unsupported precise remaining-life number;
- signal → cause shortcut without evidence chain.

Final blocking policy is defined in the next stage.
