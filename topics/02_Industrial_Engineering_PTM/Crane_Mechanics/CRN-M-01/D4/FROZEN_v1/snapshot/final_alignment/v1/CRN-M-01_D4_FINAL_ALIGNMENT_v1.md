# 6. Why ER-12 is not a gap

ER-12 requires:

> transfer to an unseen problem.

If the exact verification case were taught in Practice, it would stop being unseen.

Therefore the alignment contract is intentionally:

```text
LESSON
teaches general architecture
↓
PRACTICE A–H
builds integrated capability
↓
EXPECTED RESULT
requires transfer
↓
RUBRIC
introduces unseen case + adversarial change
```

This is not missing training.

It is correct verification design.

Status:

`PASS — VERIFICATION-ONLY EVIDENCE BY DESIGN`
