## Nakayama, Shimizu, Tahara — 2025
Wire rope mis-wrap due to wire rope “cut-in” on LEBUS drums.
Journal of the Japan Society of Mechanical Engineers.
DOI: 10.1299/transjsme.24-00238

Публичный abstract:
- экспериментально исследован cut-in;
- сравнивались LEBUS и ungrooved drum;
- observed multi-wrap slip as contributing mechanism in test configuration.

Использовано:
- cut-in is dynamic package phenomenon;
- multiple wraps may move together;
- drum geometry changes mechanism.

Guard:
small experimental system ≠ universal crane coefficient.
