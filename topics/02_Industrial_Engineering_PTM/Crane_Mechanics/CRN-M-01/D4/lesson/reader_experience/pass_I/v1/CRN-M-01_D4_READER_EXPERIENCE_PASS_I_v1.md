## Reader Experience principle

> **Не упрощать предмет. Улучшать путь к предмету.**

The pass does not remove models, guards, source boundaries or expert distinctions.

# Findings and improvements

## RX-01 — D4 progression was visible to the author, but not explicit enough to the reader

**Risk:** learner experiences nine technical chapters instead of one competency climb.

### Applied

Part I now contains a whole-D4 map:

```text
SOURCE / METHOD
→ COMPATIBILITY
→ LOCAL MECHANICS
→ DAMAGE HISTORY
→ ROTATION / INSTALLATION
→ MULTILAYER PACKAGE
→ CHAIN–WHEEL
→ SPECIALIZED INSPECTION
→ SYNTHESIS
```

---

## RX-02 — Part I has the highest cognitive density

**Risk:** learner tries to memorize document names and loses the five-question operating system.

### Applied

A Reader Navigator now tells the learner what **not** to memorize and what capability to carry forward.

---

## RX-03 — Red-Team guards can dominate the reading rhythm

**Risk:** learner remembers prohibitions instead of causal models.

### Applied

Parts IV–VIII now begin with one main causal line explaining what all later guards are protecting.

Example:

```text
COUNTER
→ MODEL BRIDGE
→ DAMAGE
```

instead of treating every `≠` as an isolated rule.

---

## RX-04 — Every Part needed an explicit capability target

**Risk:** learner knows what was discussed but not what changed in their ability.

### Applied

Each Part now states:

`К концу части ты должен уметь…`

These are capability markers, not summaries.

---

## RX-05 — Parts ended with technical bridges but lacked retrieval practice

**Risk:** passive recognition masquerades as understanding.

### Applied

Every Part now ends with a short 3–5 question Reader Checkpoint before the next Part.

These questions require causal explanation, not vocabulary recall.

---

## RX-06 — English/Russian hybrid terminology can create unnecessary friction

**Risk:** reader stops to memorize labels rather than preserve distinctions.

### Applied

Part I explicitly states that labels are shorthand for engineering distinctions and can be restated in the reader's own words.

Technical terminology is not removed.

---

## RX-07 — Part IX needed to feel like synthesis, not a tenth technical layer

**Risk:** final case becomes another chapter to consume.

### Applied

Part IX now begins with a Final Navigator and ends with a capstone retrieval check.

The reader is asked to reconstruct the D4 process from memory.

---

# No technical simplification

The pass did NOT:
- remove source-boundary guards;
- remove Red-Team safety guards;
- shorten model-validity discussions;
- replace technical terms with vague analogies;
- introduce universal rules;
- remove uncertainty;
- merge different authority layers.

The technical content is intentionally still D4.

What changed is the path through it.

