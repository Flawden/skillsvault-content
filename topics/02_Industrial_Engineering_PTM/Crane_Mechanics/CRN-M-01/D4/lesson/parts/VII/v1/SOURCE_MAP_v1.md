## ISO 3077:2001

`Short-link chain for lifting purposes — Grade T, (types T, DAT and DT), fine-tolerance hoist chain`

Статус на 2026-08-31:
- Published;
- Edition 3;
- publication 2001-12;
- reviewed and confirmed in 2023;
- remains current.

Официальная страница:
https://www.iso.org/standard/33229.html

Публичная область:
- fine-tolerance hoist chain;
- use with manual or power-driven chain hoists;
- T / DAT / DT types;
- electrically welded, round, short-link, heat-treated chains.

Использовано:
- fine-tolerance hoist chain is a specific product family;
- chain identity and application matter.
