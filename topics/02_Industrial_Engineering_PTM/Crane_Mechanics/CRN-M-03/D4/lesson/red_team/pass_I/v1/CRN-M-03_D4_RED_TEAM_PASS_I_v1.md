## Adversarial question

Assume a technically strong but overconfident reader wants to obtain more certainty or authority than the evidence supports.

Can the lesson be abused so that:

- `NOT YET DEMONSTRATED` applicability becomes permission to calculate formally;
- an as-found hook/grab geometry becomes approved/design-conforming geometry;
- a plausible or higher-fidelity model deletes governing source-required load/proof cases;
- ISO 17440 conditional-extension language becomes automatic shank-hook proof permission;
- C-hook coverage or generic attachment language is laundered into crane-hook or grab/grab-bucket authority;
- two agreeing calculations hide one shared wrong input/interpretation;
- a chosen sensitivity envelope manufactures robustness;
- “no defect observed” or “no event logged” becomes “defect/event absent”;
- an analytical STOP becomes temporary permission to continue operation;
- a technical recommendation/source-gap handoff quietly becomes the authorized equipment decision?

Pass I attacks exactly those promotions of evidence/status.

# Findings and corrections

## RT-M03-D4-01 — `NOT YET DEMONSTRATED` applicability could become “probably usable”

**Severity:** HIGH
**Part:** I

Added an explicit applicability gate:

```text
PRELIMINARY SCOPE MATCH
≠
FINAL METHOD APPLICABILITY

FINAL APPLICABILITY NOT YET DEMONSTRATED
≠
PERMISSION TO USE THE METHOD AS FORMAL PROOF
≠
PERMISSION TO MAKE THE EQUIPMENT DECISION
```

Applicability is a gate, not a confidence slider.

---

## RT-M03-D4-02 — As-found hook/grab configuration could become approved configuration

**Severity:** CRITICAL
**Part:** II

An input-freeze record can accidentally give authority-looking permanence to a field-observed geometry/state.

Added:

```text
OBSERVED / AS-FOUND CONFIGURATION
≠
APPROVED / DESIGN-CONFORMING CONFIGURATION
```

The model must preserve both records when they differ; the discrepancy itself becomes an uncertainty/escalation item.

---

## RT-M03-D4-03 — Model adequacy could be used to delete governing source-required cases

**Severity:** HIGH
**Part:** III

A quasi-static or otherwise adequate model for one chosen output can still be abused to claim that governing source-defined loads/combinations/proof cases are unnecessary.

Added:

```text
MODEL FORM SUFFICIENT FOR THIS OUTPUT
≠
GOVERNING LOAD / PROOF METHOD SATISFIED
```

Model-form reasoning cannot delete source-required cases, factors, combinations or proof branches.

---

## RT-M03-D4-04 — ISO 17440 conditional extension could be promoted into automatic applicability

**Severity:** CRITICAL
**Part:** IV

The phrase “principles can be applied to other shank hooks” is dangerous if the exact conditions disappear in retelling.

Added:

```text
CONDITIONAL EXTENSION EXISTS
≠
THIS SHANK / GEOMETRY IS ELIGIBLE

ONE REAL SOURCE VALUE FOUND
≠
CORRECT METHOD BRANCH / CONDITION / UNIT / COMPONENT
```

Any conditional extension requires the legitimately available exact conditions and component mapping. Plate hooks remain excluded by the public scope. A real source value used in the wrong branch is still wrong.

---

## RT-M03-D4-05 — Attachment taxonomy could be laundered into grab or crane-hook authority

**Severity:** CRITICAL
**Part:** V

A reader can notice that ISO 17096 covers some load-lifting attachments such as C-hooks and then silently jump to crane hook suspension proof or grabs.

Added:

```text
ISO 17096 C-HOOK COVERAGE
≠
CRANE HOOK-SUSPENSION PROOF AUTHORITY

ISO 17096 LOAD-LIFTING-ATTACHMENT SCOPE
≠
GRAB / GRAB-BUCKET AUTHORITY
```

A valid parent-model reaction also does not make a source-invalid child proof method valid.

---

## RT-M03-D4-06 — Two agreeing models could share one common-mode failure

**Severity:** CRITICAL
**Part:** VI

“Independent calculation” can be only cosmetically independent if both implementations share the same geometry revision, load mapping, contact assumption, material input or source interpretation.

Added independence dimensions:

- implementation;
- input provenance;
- geometry/configuration mapping;
- model form;
- measurement/validation evidence;
- source/criterion interpretation.

```text
TWO MODELS AGREE
≠
COMMON-MODE ERROR EXCLUDED
≠
REAL-SYSTEM VALIDATION
```

---

## RT-M03-D4-07 — A defensible-looking chosen envelope could manufacture robustness

**Severity:** CRITICAL
**Part:** VII

Even sourced scenarios can be incomplete for real-world states.

Added:

```text
ROBUST ACROSS TESTED / SOURCED ENVELOPE
≠
ROBUST OUTSIDE THE ENVELOPE
≠
UNKNOWN / UNMODELED STATES EXCLUDED
```

The robustness record now requires provenance **and coverage**. Unknown coverage remains explicit.

---

## RT-M03-D4-08 — “No defect observed / no event logged” could become absence evidence

**Severity:** CRITICAL
**Part:** VIII

Inspection and monitoring both need an observability gate.

Added:

```text
NO DEFECT OBSERVED
≠
DEFECT ABSENT

NO EVENT LOGGED
≠
EVENT DID NOT OCCUR
```

Negative evidence is usable only after recording inspection access/method capability/procedure/state/competence or monitoring coverage/resolution/sensor mapping/data completeness.

---

## RT-M03-D4-09 — Analysis STOP could be misread as temporary permission to operate

**Severity:** CRITICAL
**Part:** IX

Added:

```text
ANALYSIS / PROOF STOPPED
≠
OPERATION PERMITTED UNTIL ANALYSIS FINISHES

METHOD / CAUSE UNKNOWN
≠
APPLICABLE SAFETY ACTION MAY BE DELAYED
```

Analysis status and operational status are separate channels.

---

## RT-M03-D4-10 — Technical recommendation or source-gap handoff could become the equipment decision

**Severity:** HIGH
**Part:** IX

The final D4 review record now requires:

- technical recommendation/status;
- explicit `NOT AUTHORIZING` state unless authority is actually held;
- actual decision owner/process;
- decision status/basis/date when that decision exists.

```text
TECHNICAL RECOMMENDATION
≠
AUTHORIZED EQUIPMENT DECISION

SOURCE GAP HANDOFF
≠
PERMISSION TO CONTINUE BY DEFAULT
```
