## 1. ISO 4306-1:2026

`Cranes — Vocabulary — Part 1: General`

Public ISO metadata verified 2026-09-02:

- Edition 5;
- published 2026-07;
- status: published, stage 60.60;
- covers crane vocabulary, general concepts and component parts.

Role in D1: crane-system terminology and component-role anchor.

Public metadata URL: https://www.iso.org/standard/78820.html

## 2. ISO 1837:2003

`Lifting hooks — Nomenclature`

Public ISO metadata verified 2026-09-02:

- Edition 2;
- status: published / confirmed, stage 90.93;
- establishes nomenclature for various types of hooks used on lifting equipment.

Role in D1: generic hook-nomenclature anchor.

Claim restriction: nomenclature does not by itself supply design, wear, discard, capacity or service criteria.

Public metadata URL: https://www.iso.org/standard/37599.html

## 3. ISO 17440:2014

`Cranes — General design — Limit states and proof of competence of forged steel hooks`

Public ISO metadata verified 2026-09-02:

- Edition 1;
- status: published / confirmed, stage 90.93;
- covers bodies of point hooks made from steel forgings and machined shanks with thread/nut suspension under its stated material/scope conditions;
- plate hooks are explicitly outside its scope;
- it is a proof-of-competence method boundary, not a generic beginner inspection or field-acceptance shortcut.

Role in D1: primary boundary showing that hook proof is a formal source-bound engineering problem and that “hook” is not one universal design class.

Claim restriction: public metadata is not enough to reproduce paid equations/tables, perform a real proof, or extend the standard to hook-block frames, bearings, latches, plate hooks or other excluded constructions.

Public metadata URL: https://www.iso.org/standard/59758.html

## 4. ISO 17096:2015

`Cranes — Safety — Load lifting attachments`

Public ISO metadata verified 2026-09-02:

- Edition 1;
- status: published / confirmed, stage 90.93;
- public scope includes several non-fixed lifting attachments such as plate clamps, vacuum lifters, lifting magnets, lifting beams/spreader beams, C-hooks, lifting forks and clamps;
- the public scope explicitly excludes buckets, grabs, grab buckets and container spreaders.

Role in D1: attachment-category and source-scope boundary.

Critical guard: **ISO 17096 must not be silently extended to grabs/grab buckets merely because they are discussed in the same SkillsVault topic.** Grab/grab-bucket technical criteria remain locked to their exact applicable source/device/OEM documentation.

Public metadata URL: https://www.iso.org/standard/58103.html

## 5. ФНП ПС №461

`Приказ Ростехнадзора от 26.11.2020 №461`.

Verified 2026-09-02:

- current displayed edition: 16.04.2026;
- remains the Russian industrial-safety regulatory boundary for hazardous production facilities using lifting structures;
- validity is limited through 01.09.2032.

Role in D1: regulatory/documentation and operational-authority boundary. It does not replace hook/device design standards, device manuals or passport/OEM documentation.

Reference: https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_373321/

## 6. Passport / OEM / device documentation

Required for real equipment-specific claims, including:

- actual hook suspension/block arrangement and interfaces;
- hook type, material/design branch and approved configuration;
- permitted hook/device capacity and compatibility with the crane/load;
- keeper/latch design and approved condition;
- bearings, crossheads, nuts, retainers and other suspension details;
- actual clamp/grab/magnet/vacuum device type and operating requirements;
- inspections, tests, maintenance, replacement and repair requirements;
- any field acceptance, modification or return-to-service decision.

This source is equipment/device-specific and is not assumed to be publicly distributable.

## Authoring guards

D1 authoring MUST NOT invent or infer from public metadata:

- numeric hook geometry or discard limits;
- wear/deformation/crack acceptance thresholds;
- material properties or proof coefficients;
- side-load, tip-load or eccentricity limits;
- latch/keeper strength or retrofit rules;
- attachment/device capacity formulas presented as operational limits;
- friction coefficients, magnetic/vacuum holding margins or grab closure-force criteria;
- load-test values or acceptance rules;
- repair/NDT decisions;
- equipment-specific permission to continue operation.

For grabs/grab buckets, the ISO 17096 exclusion is treated as a **scope warning**, not as permission to fill the gap from analogy.

## Result

The source set is sufficient to author a safe beginner D1 mental model while keeping all numeric/design/acceptance/repair/authority claims locked.

**D1 SOURCE GATE = COMPLETE / PASS WITH CLAIM LIMITS.**
