# CRN-M-03 / D1 Practice v1 — Alignment Map

## Purpose

Practice A–J checks transfer from the reviewed beginner lesson into new hook-suspension and load-gripping-device situations.

It introduces no new technical authority. The learner must identify the object, map load transfer, classify the holding principle, separate observation from unknown/evidence status, route the question to the correct source role and stop where D1 evidence is insufficient.

## Required D1 capabilities

Across A–J the learner must demonstrate:

1. distinction between hook body, hook suspension and detachable below-hook attachment;
2. explicit system boundary selection;
3. load-path mapping that may branch and merge;
4. refusal to infer equal load sharing from visual symmetry;
5. separation of hook-body role from latch/keeper role;
6. qualitative seating/contact reasoning without numeric acceptance limits;
7. holding-principle classification by causal mechanism rather than silhouette;
8. observation / history / hypothesis / unknown / technical-decision status separation;
9. exact-device/configuration evidence requests;
10. source-role selection before exact applicability claims;
11. preservation of the ISO 17096 grab/grab-bucket exclusion;
12. treatment of attachment self-weight as part of the suspended system without invented capacity arithmetic;
13. refusal to infer interchangeability from similarity or one nominal marking;
14. refusal to turn past successful lifting into current validation/acceptance;
15. bounded conclusion and explicit STOP/evidence handoff;
16. separation of SkillsVault analysis STOP from real equipment-operation authority.
