## Coverage contract

Practice A–J должен в совокупности требовать:

1. observation / inference / unknown separation;
2. causal chains instead of labels;
3. mechanically distinct competing hypotheses only where non-uniqueness remains;
4. D2 model rebuild per hypothesis;
5. falsifiable and condition-matched predictions;
6. observability-aware negative evidence;
7. evidence independence / circularity checks;
8. state / transient / history separation;
9. temporal cause-versus-consequence reasoning;
10. engagement-sequence non-uniqueness;
11. source discriminating value separated from source authority;
12. bounded model-status conclusion / STOP;
13. no hazardous diagnostic experiment;
14. Russian-first learner-facing language.

## No-answer-key guard

Alignment identifies the competency and trap family only. It does not specify which hypothesis is the final cause in any case, what exact evidence must exist in reality or any permission/inspection verdict.
