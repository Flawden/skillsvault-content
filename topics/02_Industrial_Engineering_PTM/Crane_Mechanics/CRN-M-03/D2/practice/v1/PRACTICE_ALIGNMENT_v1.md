## Назначение

Practice A–J проверяет перенос D2 applied-mechanics architecture на новые неполные случаи. Она не вводит formal proof, нормативные коэффициенты, holding ratings, inspection/discard criteria или operational authority.

## Coverage invariants

Across A–J the learner must demonstrate all of the following at least once:

1. decision question before model construction;
2. body/system boundary selected before reactions;
3. FBD external effects complete or explicitly bounded by assumptions;
4. force and moment equilibrium separated correctly;
5. resultant-contact position treated as evidence-bearing, not visual;
6. centre of gravity separated from geometric centre;
7. full suspended-system self-weight represented mechanically without universal capacity arithmetic;
8. articulation/constraint state treated as load-state dependent;
9. load-share problem classified as equilibrium-solvable, compatibility/stiffness dependent or unknown-contact-state;
10. geometric symmetry kept separate from stiffness/engagement/load-share equality;
11. holding-principle mechanics separated from device rating/acceptance;
12. quasistatic status justified rather than inferred from low speed;
13. translational dynamics not treated as the whole rigid-body transient model;
14. formal proof / inspection / safe-use / equipment decision roles separated;
15. exact source/OEM/equipment data requested when the mechanical model cannot answer a stronger claim;
16. Russian-first learner language preserved;
17. bounded conclusion cannot outrun the evidence chain;
18. integrated transfer demonstrated in Case J.

## Claim locks

The following remain intentionally absent:

- reconstruction of paid-source tables, factors or branch logic;
- numeric hook/component proof verdict;
- standard dynamic/load-combination factors;
- allowable eccentricity / side-load / tip-load limits;
- WLL/SWL/capacity selection for a real crane configuration;
- clamp/magnet/vacuum/grab holding-force rating;
- inspection/discard/load-test acceptance criteria;
- repair/NDT/return-to-service decision;
- rigging/sling design authority;
- permission to operate a real lifting appliance.

## Separation from Practice Reasoning

`CRN-M-03_D2_PRACTICE_v1.md` contains prompts and incomplete evidence only. Worked reasoning paths are intentionally absent.

The next stage — `D2 PRACTICE REASONING / v1` — may show multiple acceptable models, stop points, discriminating-evidence requests and bounded conclusions. It must not rewrite Practice into one deterministic answer key.
