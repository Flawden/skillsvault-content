## Critical applicability guards

### 1. General reducer scope is not exact crane-reducer applicability

ГОСТ 31592-2012 remains действующим and remains useful as a general-purpose reducer scope anchor. Its allowance to use requirements for special-purpose reducers does not prove that a generic value applies to the actual crane reducer.

```text
GENERAL REDUCER STANDARD
≠
EXACT MODEL / CONFIGURATION / VALUE AUTHORITY
```

### 2. Gear coupling is not every coupling

ГОСТ Р 50895-2025 remains действующим from 15.01.2026 and replaces ГОСТ Р 50895-96. Its published scope is gear couplings. D1 therefore preserves the existing guard:

```text
GEAR COUPLING SCOPE
≠
ELASTOMERIC / DISC / CHAIN / RIGID / OTHER COUPLING SCOPE
```

No numeric torque, speed, displacement, fit, assembly or acceptance value is transferred across coupling families.

### 3. ISO 14691 remains a watchlist companion, not crane authority

ISO 14691:2008 remains published and confirmed at stage 90.93. Its title and abstract continue to place it in petroleum, petrochemical and natural-gas industries. The standard also does not define criteria for selecting coupling types for specific applications.

Therefore D1 keeps:

```text
ISO 14691 CURRENT
AND
ISO 14691 SCOPE-LIMITED
```

This is not a contradiction. Current status does not erase scope.

## Design / rating / lubrication / condition boundaries

- ISO 6336-1:2019 remains current after its 2025 confirmation and remains an expert gear-design/rating boundary, not a beginner calculation shortcut.
- ISO 10825-1:2022 remains a condition-description and nomenclature source; it does not convert observed damage into cause, acceptance or serviceability verdict.
- ISO 12925-1:2024 remains the current enclosed-gear lubricant specification boundary; actual lubricant grade, viscosity, compatibility, quantity, interval and substitution remain equipment/OEM questions.
- ISO 4306-1:2026 remains a vocabulary/component-context anchor only.
