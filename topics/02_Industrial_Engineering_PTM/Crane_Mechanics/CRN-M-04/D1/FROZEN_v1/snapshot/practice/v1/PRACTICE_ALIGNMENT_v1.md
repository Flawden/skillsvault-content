# CRN-M-04 / D1 Practice v1 — Alignment Map

## Purpose

Practice A–J проверяет перенос reviewed beginner lesson на новые ситуации с редукторами и муфтами без расширения технических полномочий.

Ученик должен выбрать границу привода, построить bounded drive-path, различить функцию/family/exact identity, отделить evidence от diagnosis/acceptance, выбрать source role и остановиться там, где D1 evidence недостаточно.

## Required D1 capabilities

Across A–J the learner must demonstrate:

1. explicit drive-system boundary selection;
2. distinction between observed connectivity and proven internal architecture;
3. refusal to infer universal direction of rotation/power flow from a teaching arrow;
4. separation of kinematic ratio, actual transmitted torque and permissible/rated torque;
5. reducer-family recognition without design/selection authority;
6. refusal to infer equal planetary load sharing from multiple engagements;
7. coupling function/family recognition without numeric alignment acceptance;
8. exact-device/configuration evidence request before interchangeability claims;
9. preservation of the ГОСТ Р 50895-2025 gear-coupling scope boundary;
10. lubrication observation/history/source-status separation;
11. preservation of the ISO 12925-1 specification-boundary guard;
12. condition evidence != diagnosis / acceptance / remaining-life verdict;
13. damage nomenclature != root-cause proof;
14. source-role selection before exact applicability claims;
15. bounded STOP / exact-evidence handoff;
16. separation of SkillsVault analytical STOP from real equipment-operation authority.
