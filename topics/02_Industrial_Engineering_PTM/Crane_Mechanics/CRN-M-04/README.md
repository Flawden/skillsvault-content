## Topic mission

Build a progressive mental model of crane drive reducers and couplings from beginner component/function recognition through mechanics, diagnostic reasoning and expert method governance without turning SkillsVault into unsourced design, maintenance or operational authority.

- **D2** — mechanics: ratios, torque/power/reactions and component interfaces under source-bound assumptions.
- **D3** — causal diagnosis: evidence patterns, competing failure mechanisms, history and bounded troubleshooting reasoning.
- **D4** — expert/reference method governance: rating/verification/validation, source applicability, uncertainty and authority.
