## Known-case coverage

- Practice A–J is supporting evidence, not transfer proof.
- Practice Reasoning gives multiple valid paths and deliberate STOP outcomes; it is not a single answer key.
- Integrated Case J is known and therefore cannot close ER-D2-13.
- Cases D/E/G/H/I demonstrate that correct STOP can be stronger than invented arithmetic.

## Transfer requirement

Verification must include:
- unseen U1 combining at least four D2 trap families;
- C1 that changes evidence status after an initial model;
- a complete evidence package;
- at least one opportunity where a correct bounded STOP is possible.

U1/C1 must test whether learner updates the model when evidence changes rather than protecting a prior answer.

## Candidate failure provenance

All 12 `R-F01…R-F12` families from Practice Reasoning are handed downstream as **candidate** blocking failures. Expected Result does not decide final non-compensable classification.

`SR-CAND-D2-01` covers non-discriminating evidence requests and is handed as severity-review material.

## Authority boundary

Expected Result does not unlock gear rating, reducer/coupling selection, service factors, alignment acceptance, bearing life, repair, or equipment-operation permission.
